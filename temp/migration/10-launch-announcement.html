1. E-mail to the team

Subject: The new SouthPoll.com member site is live — please try it today

Hi Kathy, Paul and Nathan,

The new member site is live on southpoll.com. The old classifieds plugin is switched off and everything members do now goes through one place: Member Login in the site menu.

What changed for members

  • Log in with the e-mail address on their SPGCA record and the password they already had. Anyone who has never logged in, or whose account was created from the registry export, uses Forgot your password? on the login page.
  • The dashboard holds the farm page, classified listings, membership and payments, favorites, saved searches and account details. A Help Center in the dashboard menu answers the common how-to questions.
  • Membership end dates now match the registry export: most memberships run to December 31, 2026. Renewal reminders go out 30, 7 and 1 days before, and members can renew by card or tell us they paid by check.
  • Listing classes are now Bulls, Cows, Heifers, Steers and Semen. Existing listings were moved into the right class.
  • Farm pages have the field names Nathan asked for (Primary Forage Type, Cover Crops, Supplemental Feed, Hay Type, Grazing Style, Additional Info) and a "Member directory" button at the top next to Favorite.

What I'd like each of you to try today

  • Kathy: open a few farm pages from the Member Directory, use the directory button at the top, and check your own farm page and membership screen read correctly.
  • Paul: open My Listings, edit a listing, press Publish (not "Save draft"; a draft stays private and the editor now says so in plain words), then check it shows in the classifieds.
  • Nathan: try the things that used to produce the small grey errors (a listing with no title, a wrong password) and confirm the new error boxes are impossible to miss; check the farm field labels and the listing classes.
  • Everyone: log in once. A welcome popup explains the soft launch; close it with Got it.

If anything looks wrong

Press the Feedback / report a problem button in the bottom-right corner of the login page or any dashboard page. Say what you expected and what happened; the page and your browser are attached automatically and you can add a screenshot. Every message comes straight to me by e-mail and is kept under SouthPoll → Feedback in the admin area. Please use it rather than texting me; it keeps the list in one place.

For the office

  • A registry digest e-mail arrives at registry@southpoll.com each morning at 8:00 Central on days when memberships were added, renewed, expired or cancelled, with a CSV attached. Quiet days send nothing.
  • 37 rows in the registry export had no e-mail address and could not be matched to a web account; the list is in the registry-unmatched CSV I'll send separately. Those members can still be added by hand under SouthPoll → Users.
  • Admin screens: SouthPoll → Users / Farms / Listings / Memberships / Feedback in the WordPress admin.

Known items still on my list: the public Website FAQ page text is being replaced this week; a handful of farms have no street address on file and therefore no map pin.

Thanks for the testing so far; it shaped most of what shipped today.

Carson

2. Newsletter block for members

The new SouthPoll.com member site is here

Your SPGCA membership, your farm page and the South Poll classifieds now live in one place. Click Member Login at the top of southpoll.com and sign in with the e-mail address on your SPGCA record. First time, or not sure of your password? Use Forgot your password? and follow the link we send you.

Once you're in you can update your farm page (it's listed in the Member Directory and on the map), post classified listings under Bulls, Cows, Heifers, Steers or Semen, renew your membership by card, and save favorite farms and searches so we can e-mail you when something new is listed. Buyers who aren't members can create a free account to contact sellers and set up alerts.

The Help Center inside the member area walks through every screen. And because this is a brand-new site, we want to hear how it works for you: use the Feedback button in the corner of any member page to tell us what's confusing, what's broken or what you'd like added. Every message is read, and your suggestions will shape the improvements we make over the coming weeks.

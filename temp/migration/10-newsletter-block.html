Your SPGCA member portal just got a whole lot better

We're excited to announce the relaunch of the membership and classifieds portal on SouthPoll.com. The rest of the site is the same place you know; what's new is everything behind Member Login, rebuilt from the ground up around what you told us you needed to run your operation and sell your cattle.

What's new for your farm

  • Everything in one place. Your membership, farm page, listings and account details sit on one dashboard that works as well from the truck as from the office.
  • A farm page that sells your program. Your logo, banner, a photo gallery, visiting hours, your forage and grazing details, and a pin on the member map. Buyers can message you straight from the page, and your contact details are shown only to logged-in, verified accounts.
  • Listings that take minutes, not an afternoon. Post under Bulls, Cows, Heifers, Steers or Semen with photos and video, mark animals sale pending or sold with one click, and renew a listing in one click when it's about to expire. Pause a listing instead of deleting it and bring it back later.
  • Buyers come to you. Members who favorite your farm or save a search are e-mailed the moment you post something that matches, so a new listing reaches interested buyers the same day.
  • Renewals without the paperwork. Renew by card in minutes, turn on automatic renewal if you'd rather not think about it, or tell us when you've mailed a check. Reminders arrive well before anything lapses, and your farm page and listings stay up as long as you're current.
  • Bring in a hand. Invite a spouse, partner or ranch manager as an account manager; they can keep the farm page and listings current while the membership stays in your name.
  • Help when you need it. A built-in Help Center walks through every screen, and the office is one click away.

Getting in

Click Member Login at the top of southpoll.com and sign in with the e-mail address on your SPGCA record. If you've never logged in, or can't remember your password, use Forgot your password? and follow the link we send you. Your membership dates are already in the system from the registry, so there's nothing to re-enter.

Help us make it better

A new portal is a work in progress, and the people who know what a cattle operation needs are the ones using it. Use the Feedback button in the corner of any member page to tell us what's confusing, what's broken or what you'd like to see. Every message is read, and your suggestions will shape the improvements we roll out over the coming weeks.

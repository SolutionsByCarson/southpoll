Intro block

Welcome to the new SouthPoll.com member site

Your SPGCA membership, farm page and classified listings now live in one place: the Member Login at the top of the site. Log in with the e-mail address SPGCA has on file. New here? Create a free buyer account to contact sellers, save favorites and get alerts, or a member account to list your farm and sell South Poll cattle.

Accordion items

1. How do I log in?

Click Member Login in the menu and enter the e-mail address on your SPGCA record and your password. Your e-mail is your login; there is no separate username.

If you have never logged in to the new site, click Forgot your password? on the login page, enter your e-mail and follow the link we send you to choose a password (check your spam folder if it does not arrive within a few minutes).

2. How do I join or renew my membership?

Log in and open My Membership. Press Pay by card to pay the Annual Membership ($100 per year) on our secure checkout; the membership is active the moment the payment goes through and you receive a receipt by e-mail. Paying by check? Choose I paid by check / cash on the same screen and the office activates the membership once the check arrives.

We remind you by e-mail 30, 7 and 1 days before the membership ends. Turn Renew automatically on and your card is charged on the end date; otherwise renew from My Membership before that date to keep your farm page and listings visible.

3. How do I set up my farm page?

Open My Farm on your dashboard. Fill in the farm name and slogan, your "about" text, address (this places your pin on the map), phone, e-mail and website, social links, visiting hours and what you offer, then add a logo, a banner and gallery photos. Press Save farm page. Your farm is listed in the Member Directory while your membership is active; contact details are shown only to logged-in, verified accounts, and visitors can message you through the contact form.

4. How do I publish a classified listing?

Open My Listings and press New listing. Give it a title, tick the class (Bulls, Cows, Heifers, Steers or Semen), enter a price or leave it blank for "Call for price", write the details, add a featured photo and any extra photos or a video, then press Publish. "Save draft" keeps your work but buyers cannot see a draft.

Listings run for 60 days; we e-mail you a week before one expires with a one-click Renew. You can have up to 10 live listings at a time.

5. How do I edit, pause or mark a listing sold?

Everything is on My Listings: Edit opens the listing, Pause hides it without deleting anything (press Publish to show it again), and the status menu lets you mark it Sale pending or Sold. Sold listings stay visible with a Sold badge for 14 days and then hide themselves. Copy makes a new draft from an existing listing.

6. How do I update my account details or let someone else help?

Account Details is where you change your name, phone, address, e-mail and password. If someone else looks after your farm page or listings, invite them as an account manager from My Farm → Users: they log in with their own account and can edit the farm and listings, while only you (the owner) can pay for, renew or cancel the membership.

7. Something is not working — who do I ask?

Press the Feedback / report a problem button in the bottom-right corner of the login page or any member page. Tell us what you expected and what happened; the page and your browser are sent along and you can attach a screenshot. Every message reaches the team by e-mail. Questions about registrations, transfers and the cattle registry itself go to the SPGCA office: registry@southpoll.com · 816-629-8010.

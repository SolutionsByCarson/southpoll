What to test

  1. Member directory — southpollgrstg.wpenginepowered.com/members/
    Search by farm or owner name, filter by state and region, switch to the map, click a pin, open a farm page. Does your farm show the right name, address, logo, photos and contact details?
  2. Classifieds — southpollgrstg.wpenginepowered.com/classifieds/
    Filter by category and state, try the map (pins show the listings at each farm), open a listing. If you had listings on the old site they should all be here, with photos and “sold” marks.
  3. Your dashboard — southpollgrstg.wpenginepowered.com/members/dashboard/
    Check the membership status box, days remaining, and the quick cards.
  4. My Farm — southpollgrstg.wpenginepowered.com/members/my-farm/
    Edit your farm details, upload a logo, banner and gallery photos, set what you offer (bulls, females, semen…), add an account manager, save. Then open your public farm page and confirm the changes show.
  5. My Listings — southpollgrstg.wpenginepowered.com/members/my-listings/
    Add a new listing with photos, publish it, mark one as sold, renew an expiring one. Check it appears in the classifieds.
  6. Manage Membership — southpollgrstg.wpenginepowered.com/members/manage-membership/
    • Renew online with Stripe’s test card 4242 4242 4242 4242, any future expiry date, any 3-digit code, any ZIP. No real money moves.
    • “I paid by check or cash”: submit a claim, then (office) approve it under SouthPoll → Memberships → Claims in the admin.
    • Turn automatic renewal on and off, and try “Cancel membership”. Cancelling just stops automatic renewal; the membership runs to its end date. When auto-renew is already off the button is disabled and the page tells you the expiry date.
  7. Favorites and saved searches (log in as demo-buyer) — my-favorites and saved-searches
    Favorite a farm and a listing, save a search from the classifieds, apply it from the “saved searches” dropdown, remove it.
  8. Contact forms — on a farm page and on a listing, send a message as a visitor (logged out). The farm owner’s email receives it; test emails on staging may land in spam folders.
  9. Old links — bookmarks and emails with old addresses should land on the right new page, e.g. /my-account/, /my-account/my-store/, /listings/, or an old /store/… or /rtcl_listing/… address.
  10. Join page — southpollgrstg.wpenginepowered.com/join-spgca/ → “Join Today Via Online” → create a new account → activate a membership with the test card. Use a real email address you can read; the verification email goes there.

For the office (Kathy): the admin side

Log in to southpollgrstg.wpenginepowered.com/wp-admin/ with your usual staff account, then open the SouthPoll menu:

  • Users / Farms / Listings — search, filter, open a record, export CSV. Spot-check a few members you know.
  • Memberships — the Claims tab (approve a check payment), extend a term, record an offline payment, cancel.
  • Settings — notification addresses, renewal reminder days, and the new Cattle registry digest: the Monday and Thursday 8 am email listing who to add to and remove from the registry. Press “Send registry digest now” on the Status page to get one immediately and check the format and the attached CSV.
  • Status — shows the scheduled jobs and lets you send a test email.
